# Notation and Conventions

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `S_0`, `S_t`, `S_T` | Spot price now, at time `t`, and at maturity `T` |
| `K` | Strike or contractual delivery price |
| `F_0(T)` | Forward price agreed now for maturity `T` |
| `C`, `P` | European call and put prices now |
| `r` | Continuously compounded risk-free rate unless stated otherwise |
| `q` | Continuous income or dividend yield |
| `sigma` | Annualized volatility parameter |
| `N(x)` | Standard normal cumulative distribution function |
| `Delta` | Units of underlying in a local replicating hedge |

Money values must name their currency; time is measured in years. Positive cash flow means received by the portfolio owner. European options exercise only at maturity. The initial chapters assume frictionless borrowing, lending, and trading at quoted prices; later excavations remove those assumptions.
