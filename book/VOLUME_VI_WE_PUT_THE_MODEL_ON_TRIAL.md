## Coda — The reader takes the desk

At the beginning, Mara and Arun needed one promise. At the end, the reader can take an unfamiliar term sheet and expose its state variables, cash-flow timing, replication, pricing choices, numerical evidence, hedge, liquidity path, and failure modes.

That competence still does not reveal a universal true value. It reveals something more useful: which part of value is forced by tradable consistency, which part depends on a model, which part depends on real-world belief, and which part belongs to the holder's need for protection or capacity to bear risk.

The graduate does not say “this put is cheap” after seeing one volatility number. They say which executable price they can trade, which assumptions produce a valuation range, which costs and risks remain, why buyer and seller may rationally differ, and what evidence would falsify the conclusion.
