## Coda — Every omitted line returns as basis

The carry ledger gathers financing, income, storage, and convenience. Basis records the remaining difference between a real exposure and standardized hedge. Margin forces future economics into present liquidity. Regression chooses a hedge ratio from observed co-movement but cannot promise stability. Bootstrapping turns quoted instruments into dated discount factors. Swaps assemble those dates into exchanges of fixed and floating cash.

By now an unfamiliar forward should not intimidate the reader. Draw both delivery routes, record every service and cash flow, and ask whether each route is actually tradable. The equation follows.

The final volume removes the last conveniences: exercise can happen early, payoffs remember paths, numerical answers carry error, trading costs money, and risk summaries can hide the event that ends the institution.
