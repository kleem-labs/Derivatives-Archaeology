## Coda — The tear in the replication cloth

The hedge begins locally perfect and ends honestly incomplete. Delta removes a tangent exposure; gamma measures the curvature left behind. Theta and realized movement exchange value through time. Implied volatility turns market price into a coordinate, and the smile shows one constant diffusion cannot carry every observed claim. Richer volatility dynamics fit more evidence but introduce new state risk. A jump finally tears the continuous hedge.

This ending is central to independent valuation. When replication is complete, no-arbitrage can force a price. When it is incomplete, valuation needs an additional choice. A responsible reader must label that choice rather than disguising it as mathematics.

The next volume returns to things that must be financed, stored, delivered, margined, rolled, and settled. The physical world will make every omitted cash flow visible.
