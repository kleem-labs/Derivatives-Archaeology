# The Book in Six Parts

| Part | Excavations | Human question |
|---|---:|---|
| I — Promises and Prices | 000–005 | What cash promise am I actually considering, and can I survive its path? |
| II — Options and Replication | 006–011 | Am I buying a right, selling an obligation, or building a defined-risk combination? |
| III — Probability in Motion | 012–019 | Which future-price model is being used, and what does it fail to say about my belief? |
| IV — Sensitivity and Volatility | 020–029 | Which move—direction, time, uncertainty, or jump—does this strategy need or fear? |
| V — Futures, Rates, and Curves | 030–039 | Can I fund the carry, margin, delivery, and timing of this hedge or futures option? |
| VI — Trading Reality and Model Risk | 040–049 | What assignment, execution, model, and tail risks remain before I call a strategy acceptable? |

All fifty excavations are present. The [complete table of contents](TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md) gives direct chapter links; [the conclusion](CONCLUSION.md) explains why the foundational book ends at 049.

Read the parts as a route toward the [Strategy Field Guide](STRATEGY_FIELD_GUIDE.md), not as a menu of trade recommendations.
