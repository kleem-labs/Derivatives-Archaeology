# Glossary

- **Arbitrage:** a self-financing strategy with no possible loss and a possible gain, with at least one strict gain under the stated model.
- **Call:** the right, not obligation, to buy an underlying at a strike.
- **Derivative:** a contract whose cash flows depend on another observable quantity.
- **Forward:** an over-the-counter agreement today to transact later at a fixed delivery price.
- **Futures contract:** a standardized, margined contract whose gains and losses are settled repeatedly.
- **Hedge:** a position chosen to offset a specified exposure; it need not remove every risk.
- **Numeraire:** the asset used as the unit in which other values are measured.
- **Put:** the right, not obligation, to sell an underlying at a strike.
- **Replication:** constructing the same cash flows as a claim using other traded instruments.
- **Risk-neutral probability:** pricing weights under which discounted tradable prices are martingales; not a statement of real-world beliefs.
- **Volatility:** a scale for price variability; its precise definition depends on the model or estimator.
