# Mastery Ledger

Mark an item only with attached evidence: derivation, code, payoff table, decision memo, or oral explanation recorded from memory.

## Contract literacy

- [ ] I can turn a term sheet into dated state-contingent cash flows.
- [ ] I can distinguish payoff, price, value, P&L, notional, premium, and margin.
- [ ] I can find ambiguous observation, exercise, settlement, and disruption terms.

## Pricing foundations

- [ ] I can derive forward carry and put–call parity without looking them up.
- [ ] I can replicate a one-period claim and explain risk-neutral weights without calling them forecasts.
- [ ] I can derive the logic—not merely quote the result—behind the Black–Scholes PDE.
- [ ] I can state when no-arbitrage gives one price, bounds, or no practical trade.

## Risk and computation

- [ ] I can explain every first- and second-order Greek in units and P&L terms.
- [ ] I can price the same vanilla claim by closed form, tree, and Monte Carlo and explain discrepancies.
- [ ] I test convergence, bounds, parity, and an independent implementation.
- [ ] I distinguish sampling error, parameter uncertainty, model error, and execution error.

## Market reading

- [ ] I can reconstruct executable parity using bid/ask quotes and costs.
- [ ] I can interpret an implied-volatility surface without treating it as a pure forecast.
- [ ] I can write conditional call and put valuations from explicit scenarios.
- [ ] I can explain why a hedge or insurance purchase may be valuable without being statistically underpriced.

## Creation capstone

- [ ] I designed an original derivative from a named real-world exposure.
- [ ] Its term sheet makes payoff and settlement unambiguous.
- [ ] I supplied replication or bounds, model value range, hedge, costs, stresses, and residual risks.
- [ ] I wrote fair buyer and seller memos from the same evidence.
- [ ] Another person reproduced my cash flows and valuation without asking what I meant.

Completion of every box is the book's end goal. It is evidence of independent reasoning, not a promise of profitable trading.
