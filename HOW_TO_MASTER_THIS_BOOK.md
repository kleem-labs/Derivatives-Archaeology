# How to Master Derivatives Archaeology

Reading creates recognition. Mastery requires retrieval, construction, and adversarial testing.

## Pass 1 — Discover

Read one excavation without memorizing formulas. Before the repair is revealed, state what you would try. Record why the naive attempt fails. Walk its [Memory Palace](MEMORY_PALACE.md) chamber immediately afterward.

## Pass 2 — Rebuild

Close the chapter. Recreate the payoff table, cash-flow timeline, replication argument, and equation from the market problem. Run the matching laboratory and change one input. Predict direction before observing output.

## Pass 3 — Retrieve and transfer

One day later, use only the chamber seal to explain pressure → failure → repair. One week later, transfer the idea to a different underlying or contract. Put–call parity understood only for one stock example is not yet portable knowledge.

## Pass 4 — Design

Enter the [Derivative Design Studio](DERIVATIVE_DESIGN_STUDIO.md). Begin from a real risk, not a product name. Write the payoff, settlement, observation source, dates, collateral, disruption rules, and counterparty obligations. Attempt static replication, dynamic replication, and arbitrage bounds before selecting a model.

## Pass 5 — Read a market

Use [Market Reading and Value](MARKET_READING_AND_VALUE.md). Convert bid and ask into implied parameters, compare them with conditional scenarios, include all costs, and write the falsifiable reason the trade might have positive value. “The option looks cheap” is not evidence.

## Graduation evidence

Complete the [Mastery Ledger](MASTERY_LEDGER.md). Graduation requires designing one original derivative and writing two opposing investment memos—buyer and seller—using the same facts. If both sides cannot be explained fairly, the uncertainty has not been understood.
